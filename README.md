Overview

Peatlands are one of the world’s largest terrestrial carbon stores, and their stability depends heavily on soil food web dynamics. This project investigates how short-term climate perturbations (temperature and moisture changes) impact:

Soil food web structure
Carbon (C) and nitrogen (N) fluxes
System resistance and resilience

The original work used a controlled mesocosm experiment to simulate future climate conditions and evaluate ecosystem responses. Results showed that soil communities lack resistance to environmental perturbations, with implications for carbon storage under climate change .
